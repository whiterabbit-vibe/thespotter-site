Questions

Does Spotter upload my files?

No. Everything Spotter reads and stores stays on your Mac, encrypted. Searching works offline.

How is it different from Spotlight?

Spotlight is great for opening apps and finding files by name. Spotter is built for finding files by what's in them and what you remember about them, explains every match, asks when your search is unclear, and lets you rename, move and organise results safely with undo.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple silicon running macOS 26 or later. Features that use Apple Intelligence need it turned on; search works without it.

Will it change my files without asking?

Never. Spotter only changes files when you ask, shows a preview first, never overwrites anything, and keeps an undo history.
