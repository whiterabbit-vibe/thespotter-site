    Questions, answered.

    Does Spotter upload my files?

    No. Everything Spotter reads and stores stays on your Mac, encrypted. Searching works offline. Cloud AI is off unless you turn it on, and even then only the passages a question needs are sent, after Spotter shows you what they are.

    How is it different from Spotlight?

    Spotlight is great for launching apps and finding files by name. Spotter is built for finding files by what's in them and what you remember about them. It explains every match, answers questions about your documents, searches old versions, cleans up storage and lets you change many files at once with undo.

    Does it replace Finder?

    You can use it as your everyday file manager, with libraries, every view, tabs and dual pane. The Desktop, Open and Save dialogs stay with Finder, and "Show in Finder" is always one click away.

    Do I need Apple Intelligence?

    No. Search, text recognition, storage cleanup, browsing and actions all work without it. Summaries and answers need Apple Intelligence turned on, or a cloud AI you choose.

    Why does it ask for Full Disk Access?

    Full Disk Access lets Spotter search your whole Mac. It's optional: you can pick just the folders you want instead, and change that any time.

    Will it change or delete my files?

    Only when you ask. Every change is shown as a plan first, nothing is ever overwritten, deleting always means the Trash, and every change can be undone.

    Which Macs does it run on?

    Macs with Apple silicon running a recent version of macOS. We'll confirm the exact version at launch.

    Is it a subscription?

    No. Pay once and keep it, with free updates. You can try it free first.
