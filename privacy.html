Privacy

Your files stay on your Mac.

Last updated 3 November 2026.

The Spotter app

  • Spotter reads only the folders you choose, plus your Photos library if you turn that on.
  • Everything it extracts (file details, text, text recognised in images, photo locations) is stored on your Mac in an index encrypted with AES-256. The key is kept in your Keychain.
  • Spotter does not upload your files or their contents. Search, text recognition and place names all work offline.
  • Cloud AI is off by default. If you turn it on, Spotter shows you the passages a question needs before anything is sent.
  • Spotter only changes files when you ask, shows a preview first and keeps an undo history.
  • You can remove a folder from Spotter, or delete everything it stored, at any time in Settings. Your files are not affected.

This website

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party trackers. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and reliability.
